# Competitive Positioning

## Approved Message

Atlas CRM should be positioned as a local-first, workflow-friendly CRM with strong support operations integration. #sales #positioning

Sales may say that Atlas CRM provides clearer support handoff, account timeline visibility, and configurable enterprise controls than generic CRM tools.

## Claims to Avoid

Do not claim that Atlas CRM is always faster than every competitor, has zero downtime, or can process restricted customer data through any external LLM. Avoid naming unsupported competitor weaknesses.

## Proof Points

Use customer-approved case studies, deployment architecture diagrams, and documented product capabilities from [[products/Atlas CRM]]. Data governance claims must align with [[policies/Data Handling Policy]].
